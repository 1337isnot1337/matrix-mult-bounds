# From the first A4 gap to a cap23 linear-space bound

Base: PR142 at `3d24db7`. Its written first-gap classification gives a
sharp vector linear-space dimension eight at source rank cap20, leaving
the next cap21 open. The shared-correction target-ruling criterion has
room to use the same dimension bound through cap23 if it can be proved.

Question: prove a sharp vector linear-space bound eight for all canonical
A4 source slices of rank at most23. This would exclude every balanced
long-map leading rank at least102 in A4 to M(10,10,11), with arbitrary
finite poles in the other maps and original unit lift.

The proposed structural step is to rule out a positive degree-one kernel
generator in the prior biform multiplication kernel when its primitive
x-degree is at least two. Such a generator gives a symmetric4-by4 Hankel
matrix pencil. Adjugates handle rank three; second compounds and a common
cubic annihilator handle rank two. The latter case should reduce to one
fixed support scheme of length at most two and hence only constant-generated
syzygies. Rank-one pencils must be checked separately.

The prior splitting formula would then force every cap23 biform into
one of three closed factor families: a quadratic x divisor, a quadratic
z divisor, or a linear divisor on each side. The generic-pencil common-factor
lemma controls their linear spaces. A rank24 witness in every full
nine-dimensional fixed double-linear-factor space must exclude equality
there, leaving the sharp dimension eight already attained at rank20.

Acceptance requires complete written proofs for the pencil and divisor
steps, exact source pins, literal source and kernel-module controls,
integer/algebraic rank witnesses, the cap24 dimension-twelve boundary,
focused regressions and full required CI. Finite absence of intermediate
ranks is not an accepted premise. No full degeneration exclusion, matrix
construction, independent review, formalization or exponent gain is claimed.
