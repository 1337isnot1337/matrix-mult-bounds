# Hankel syzygies, source cap23 and singular long-map rank102

The [written, unreviewed proof](proof.md) gives a sharp vector linear-space
dimension bound eight for canonical A4 source slices of rank at most23.
It extends PR142's cap20 bound and excludes the full A4 to M(10,10,11)
Laurent degeneration whenever either scalar-balanced long-map leading
rank is at least102. Both other maps and the original identity lift may
have arbitrary finite poles. Remaining candidates must have both balanced
long-map leading ranks at most101.

The structural step excludes a positive degree-one generator in the
primitive biform multiplication kernel when its x-degree is at least two.
A symmetric Hankel pencil, its adjugate and second compound, and the
existing Hermite jet geometry give an elementary proof. The prior splitting
formula then places every cap23 biform in a quadratic-divisor family or
in the family with a linear divisor on each side. A complete
nine-dimensional double-linear-factor space contains a rank24 witness,
so the largest cap23 linear space still has dimension eight.

At cap24 there is a twelve-dimensional fixed-linear-divisor source space,
so the uniform source-dimension hypothesis used here fails. No assertion
about all remaining singular degenerations or a smaller exponent follows.
The pointwise source rank21 is excluded, while rank23 points occur; the
linear-space bound is the relevant assertion.
