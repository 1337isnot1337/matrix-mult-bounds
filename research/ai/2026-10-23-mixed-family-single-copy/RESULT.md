# The displayed `T_4` interpolation factors do not lie on a twisted cubic

October 23, 2026. Exact finite calculation with a written incidence argument.
This narrows one route to the single-copy degeneration; it is not a general
obstruction and does not improve the exponent.

## Result

The border-rank-10 interpolation certificate for `T_4` uses the ten shared
linear forms given by the leading degree-three parts of

\[
\ell_{ij}(s,t)=\binom{s}{i}\binom{t}{j}\binom{3-s-t}{3-i-j},
\qquad i+j\le3.
\]

After multiplying each form by the nonzero scalar `i! j! (3-i-j)!` and
choosing a projective sign, its coefficient vector in the ordered basis
`(t^3, st^2, s^2t, s^3)` is

\[
w_{ij,x}=(-1)^{3-i-j}\binom{3-i-j}{x-i},
\]

with the binomial coefficient zero outside `0<=x-i<=3-i-j`.

Evaluate the ten quadratic monomials `X_p X_q`, `0<=p<=q<=3`, at these ten
projective points. In the recorded lexicographic orders, the resulting
integer matrix has determinant exactly `1`. Therefore no nonzero quadratic
form in the shared four-space vanishes at all ten points. The points are
projectively distinct.

View `C(4,10)` as univariate polynomial multiplication
`S^3 x S^9 -> S^12`. Evaluation at ten distinct scalars is an isomorphism
on `S^9`; projecting `S^12` to those ten values gives a restriction with
ten rank-one terms. Its shared projective factors are
`[1:tau:tau^2:tau^3]`. Thus they lie on the standard rational normal cubic.
The target's ten-term interpolation family likewise has invertible
evaluation maps on its two ten-dimensional input spaces for nonzero
parameter, with the ten displayed `w_ij` as its shared factors.

Every projective transform of the rational normal cubic
`[1:t:t^2:t^3]` lies on three independent quadrics. The source's standard
ten-node evaluation restriction has shared factors on that curve. Moreover,
if those ten projective factors vary with the parameter and converge
pointwise to ten distinct limits, properness of the Grassmannian of
three-dimensional quadratic spaces gives a nonzero limiting quadric through
all ten limits. The determinant certificate rules this out for the displayed
`T_4` interpolation factors.

Thus the source's ten-node evaluation decomposition cannot be matched
term-by-term, even through a varying common shared-leg change of basis, to
the ten factors in this particular target interpolation certificate.

## Scope

This does not exclude other border-rank-10 decompositions of `T_4`, paths
with collisions and cancellations among rank-one terms, or degenerations
using more than ten source terms. In particular it does not exclude
`C(4,10) \unrhd T_4`. It is a route-specific negative result only.
