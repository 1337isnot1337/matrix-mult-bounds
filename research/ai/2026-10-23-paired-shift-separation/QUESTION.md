# Variable-coefficient paired-source obstruction

Can one long-leg map vary with its source column while keeping the resulting channel rank below the middle width? Study ordinary restrictions of `Q(n,m)` to `M(n,m,n)` when one long-leg map has finite-band coefficients whose column dependence has bounded shift-separation rank, and the other long-leg map is finite-band Toeplitz.

The precise result is an all-field flattening lower bound. If the combined shift-separation rank is `Delta < m`, then the residual has flattening rank at least `g_B*g_C`, even when the first-leg map is arbitrary. For fixed `m`, sublinear bandwidth and exceptional-column counts give a quadratic lower bound on the residual.

This is a scoped ordinary-map obstruction. It does not handle arbitrary variable-coefficient maps, singular Laurent cancellations, or establish an exponent improvement.
