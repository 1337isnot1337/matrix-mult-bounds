# Singular source-unit resolvent with fixed compressions

Resumed October 23, 2026 from preserved uncommitted work on
`research/singular-unit-resolvent-20261021`. The base is current main
`d833eb0`, including the regular-unit obstruction merged in PR99.
The open PRs and current research ledger were inspected before resuming.
This question is separate from the canonical leading-flag, partner-family,
and conic-curvature investigations.

For the literal cyclic convolution source A4, normalize its explicit
unimodular slice to the identity and let N be its rank-16 square-zero
coordinate slice. Can a degeneration to M(10,10,11) use the singular
source identity lift N+tI, the long map P(N+tI)^(-1), constant C with
PC=I110, and an arbitrary Laurent first map?

Deliverable: a written obstruction on a specified compression locus,
with an exact source and nonvanishing-minor certificate. This is a
construction-class question; no smaller exponent follows from an
obstruction alone. The present proof covers the nonempty open dense
locus where the explicit limiting candidate space has dimension 112.
The rank-drop locus, moving compressions and other singular identity
trajectories remain open. The next question is whether the rank condition
can be removed or its exceptional locus described.
