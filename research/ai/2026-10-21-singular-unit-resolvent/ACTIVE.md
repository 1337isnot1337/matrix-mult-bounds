Selected October 21, 2026 after checking current main 39378de5, active PRs,

Question: can a singular unit lift e_(0,0,0)+tF0 evade the regular-unit
obstruction, using constant compression pairs and the exact resolvent
B=P(tI+N)^(-1)? The selected consequence is an obstruction for all Laurent
first maps, or an explicit remaining compression locus.

The result is a written, unreviewed all-order first-map obstruction when
the explicit candidate limiting-space rank is 112. An exact minor proves
this condition on a nonempty open dense set of constant pairs PC=I110.
A source grading forces an invariant subspace of dimension indivisible
by 10, contradicting a normalized Mat10 target action. The rank-drop
locus, moving compressions and other singular unit lifts remain open.
