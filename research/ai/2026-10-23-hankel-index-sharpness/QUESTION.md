# Sharpness at the first nonlinear annihilator boundary

Base: `1c04903` (the complete source pin is in `sources.json`).
Owner-directed Codex investigation, October 23, 2026.

The prior Hankel theorem assumes `5r < 2(a+b)`. Its ordinary-source
application leaves its first single-target survivors at A10. At the
relevant boundary `(a,b,r)=(10,10,8)`, can the primitive annihilator of a
pencil actually have parameter degree two? More generally, can equality
in `(a+b-2r)*epsilon <= r` occur at every positive integer epsilon?

Deliver an explicit all-size pencil, a direct proof of its complete
polynomial kernels and exact endpoint controls. This checks whether a
proposed extension of the existing linear-annihilator argument is valid.
It does not seek or assert a matrix tensor restriction or degeneration.

The existing rank-seven 8-by-8 quadratic-pencil control retains credit.
The new question concerns equality in the degree budget, including the
10-by-10 rank-eight boundary, and arbitrary degree and rectangular size.

Follow-up: classify every linear Hankel space of rank at most r that
contains the complete constructed pencil. Can the common tangent
equations be both necessary and sufficient for this extension problem?
The answer should bound a Segre ruling anchored to this exact seed,
without assuming that other quadratic pencils are equivalent to it.
