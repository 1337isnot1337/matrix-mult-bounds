# Heisenberg(5) construction lane

Question: can the order-125 Heisenberg group algebra degenerate to
M(10,10,10) with exact strict vertex potentials? Its explicit shift/phase
decomposition gives a certified conditional omega<2.247 gate. The
[normal-coset closure](normal_coset_closure.md) now excludes every single
matrix target passing the usual source gate in this coordinate construction
class. No general complex degeneration or smaller exponent is established.

The [written derivation](source_proof.md) gives the source decomposition,
the finite two-coset class, and the broader search normalization. The first
finite experiment has 600 two-coset sets, ninety sets compatible with the
fixed first set, and 400 pair-injective unordered triples. Every triple is
rejected by a saved two-row positive integer circuit. Independent replay
checks all 400 circuits, all 841 character inner products, 312500 shift/phase
products and 600 automorphism normalizations. A known subgroup control has
125 intended terms and no extra terms. Damaged certificates are rejected.

The [all-prime full-coset argument](coset_averaging.md) separately bounds
the volume of every strict-potential construction from full noncentral
cyclic cosets by p^4. Exact controls check 648,15000,115248 subgroup triples
at p=3,5,7. The p=11 three-coset progression pilot has 38 exact two-term
rejections, consistent with that general obstruction.

The discovery scripts reuse the unchanged group-potential search imported
and pinned in the SL(2,7) package. They retain its exact accepted-witness
verifier. Optional discovery dependencies are NumPy, SciPy and python-sat;
all accepted certificate replays use integer arithmetic.

The arbitrary-color two-parallel-line pilot and seven first-projection
classes have untraced solver reports. The normal-coset closure uses no SAT
refutation: it checks 3003 balanced count pairs and 410333 unequal count
triples, then proves contradictions by affine-line pair counting. The
finite search history is retained separately and is not a premise of that
proof. All written arguments remain unreviewed and unformalized.
