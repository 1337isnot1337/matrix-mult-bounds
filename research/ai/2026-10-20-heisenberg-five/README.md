# Heisenberg(5) construction lane

Question: can the order-125 Heisenberg group algebra degenerate to
M(10,10,10) with exact strict vertex potentials? Its explicit shift/phase
decomposition gives a certified conditional omega<2.247 gate. No such
degeneration or smaller exponent is established.

The [written derivation](source_proof.md) gives the source decomposition,
the finite two-coset class, and the broader search normalization. The first
finite experiment has 600 two-coset sets, ninety sets compatible with the
fixed first set, and 400 pair-injective unordered triples. Every triple is
rejected by a saved two-row positive integer circuit. Independent replay
and source-verification controls are being added before integration.

The discovery scripts reuse the unchanged group-potential search imported
and pinned in the SL(2,7) package. They retain its exact accepted-witness
verifier. Optional discovery dependencies are NumPy, SciPy and python-sat;
all accepted certificate replays use integer arithmetic.

The arbitrary-color two-parallel-line pilot returned untraced UNSAT in
16.44 seconds. No broader exclusion follows. A separate general search
uses the central-fiber and six abelian-direction bounds, with the first
projection normalized to row and column sums two. A running report is a
checkpoint only.
