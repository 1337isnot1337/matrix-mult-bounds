# Independent coordinate compressions of the reduced outer space

October 23, 2026. Continuation of PR213.

The preceding low-rank-locus reduction identifies the full constant
compression kernel with a compression kernel of
E=G+[G,F]+FGF, a 46-dimensional space of 28 by 28 matrices.
A full M(10,10,11) target requires a rank-26 pair whose image has dimension
at most 37.

This investigation asks for the minimum image dimension when the two
deleted output coordinates and the two deleted input coordinates are
chosen independently. There are binomial(28,2)^2=142884 pairs.
The intended result is an exact finite census and an exact sharpness
witness. Through the preceding kernel reduction, a lower bound of 38
would exclude every independent coordinate compression of the complete
112-dimensional conjugation family.

This is a finite class. Arbitrary complex two-dimensional output spaces
and codimension-two input spaces remain the separate algebra question.
The finite result must not be used to exclude them.
