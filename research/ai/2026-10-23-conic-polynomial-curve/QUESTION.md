# Question and acceptance criteria

Does the mixed cubic normal class `[1:d]`, at `c=-d^3`, admit an actual
all-order incidence curve with leading map ranks fourteen and projected
source rank twelve?

The finite third-, fourth- and tenth-order results do not decide this.
Here the first boundary velocity may change while the complete first
normal coefficient remains fixed. This is weaker than preserving the
full first jet or the prescribed two-jet, and stronger than exhibiting
another finite truncation.

Acceptance requires complete polynomial maps, exact literal-source
identities for their entire product, parameter-uniform leading minors,
and exact comparison with the credited normal pair. The separate
generator is a discovery/reproduction aid. The source verifier must not
depend on its gauge formula or on a tangent/cohomology solver.

The delivered degree-one/degree-three curve satisfies these criteria for
every complex a,b,d, including d=0. The complete matrix target and an
exponent improvement are outside the established result.
