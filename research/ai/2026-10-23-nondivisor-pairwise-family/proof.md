# Binomial powers and a uniform failure of pairwise rank screens

October23,2026. Owner-directed Codex rederivation, written and unreviewed.
No historical priority, independent review, formalization or exponent gain.

## 1. Two statements and their field hypotheses

Use the maintained biform coefficient convention

    R_a(g)_((j,L),(H,r))=g_(H-j,L-r),

where0<=j,r<a and0<=H,L<2a-1. There are two results.

1. Over every characteristic-zero field, for a>=2 and1<=d<a,

       rank R_a((1+xz)^d)=a(a+d).                       (1)

2. For every d>=2, set a=2d+1,T=a(a+d),q=T-1. Let S_d be the linear
   coordinate space generated by

       x^i z^s, 0<=i<d,0<=s<a,       together with x^d z^d. (2)

   Its dimension is ad+1. Every coordinate subspace generated by at most
   two of these monomials has all its matrices of rank at most q, but
   S_d contains a slice of rank T. Thus those pairwise checks do not
   certify bounded rank of the full span.

For d=2 this is an11-dimensional support passing all55 coordinate-pair
checks at ceiling34 but containing a rank35 slice. For d=3 it is a
22-dimensional support passing all231 pair checks at ceiling69 but
containing a rank70 slice. This does not refute a dimension bound for
spaces whose *entire* span obeys the ceiling: S_d does not obey it.

## 2. An elementary binomial Toeplitz determinant

For integers d>=1,n>=0 and0<=k<=d, let

    D_n(d,k)=det[binom(d,k+i-j)]_(0<=i,j<n),

with binomial coefficients outside0,...,d equal to zero and D_0=1.
We rederive the product

    D_n(d,k)=product_(i=0)^(n-1)
               (d+i)! i! / ((k+i)! (d-k+i)!).           (3)

In particular it is a positive integer, hence nonzero in every
characteristic-zero field. No splitting or classification theorem is
used in this determinant calculation.

Here is a complete induction. The cases n=0,1 are immediate. At k=0
or k=d the matrix is triangular with diagonal1, and the product is1.
For1<=k<=d-1 use the determinant condensation identity

    D_n(d,k) D_(n-2)(d,k)
       =D_(n-1)(d,k)^2-D_(n-1)(d,k-1)D_(n-1)(d,k+1).   (4)

For this application the central(n-2)-block is invertible by induction.
Eliminate it by block Gaussian elimination. The determinant and four
adjacent corner minors are its determinant times the corresponding
2-by2 Schur-complement determinant or entries. The elementary2-by2
determinant identity yields(4), including the empty-core case n=2.
Thus(4) is justified here without an external structural input.

Write F_n(d,k) for the proposed product and m=n-1. Direct cancellation
of factorials gives

    F_m(d,k-1)F_m(d,k+1)/F_m(d,k)^2
       =k(d-k)/((k+m)(d-k+m)),

    F_(m+1)(d,k)F_(m-1)(d,k)/F_m(d,k)^2
       =m(d+m)/((k+m)(d-k+m)).

The two right sides sum to1. Hence the products satisfy(4). The positive
inductive value D_(n-2)=F_(n-2) permits division, proving D_n=F_n>0.
Since D_n is an integer determinant, the displayed rational product is
an integer as well. This proves(3) for every n,d,k in its stated range.

## 3. Literal diagonal blocks of the power slice

For g=(1+xz)^d, a nonzero entry has H-j=L-r, so

    j+L=H+r.

The nonzero output H,L ranges are0,...,a+d-1. Thus there are a(a+d)
active rows and the same number of active columns; all other rows or
columns are zero. Partition them by sigma=j+L=H+r. On level sigma,
both j and r run through the same interval

    I_sigma=[max(0,sigma-(a+d-1)), min(a-1,sigma)].

Let its first index be b, its length ell, and put

    k_sigma=sigma-2b-(ell-1).

The block entries are binom(d,sigma-j-r). Reversing the r ordering makes
this a transpose of the Toeplitz matrix in(3), with n=ell and k=k_sigma.
For low levels k_sigma=0, for middle levels it runs from0 to d, and for
high levels k_sigma=d. Thus every block has nonzero determinant by(3).

The levels partition all active rows and columns, so their dimensions
sum to a(a+d). They are all full rank, proving(1). The argument works
over Q, and injectivity of Q into every characteristic-zero field
proves the stated field extension.

## 4. Uniform entry covers for all coordinate pairs

A sum of two coordinate biforms, with arbitrary coefficients, uses at
most a(a+u) columns and a(a+v) rows, where u and v are the absolute
coordinate differences. Consequently its rank is at most

    a^2+a*min(u,v).                                     (5)

This support count also covers a zero coordinate difference, when the
bound is a^2. Zero coefficients can only remove entries. Thus each
claimed pair bound is uniform over its whole two-dimensional span.

For two coordinates from the first strip in(2), u<=d-1, so(5) is at
most T-a<T-1. For a pair involving the extra point(d,d), both absolute
differences are at most d. The same bound works unless u=v=d.

In the exceptional case, remove the common monomial factor. This shifts
only active output rows/columns without changing rank. The biform is
1+x^d z^d or x^d+z^d, up to its two coefficients. Decompose by the two
input residues modulo d. Since a=2d+1, one residue length is3 and the
other d-1 lengths are2 on each axis.

A block with input lengths b,c has at most b(c+1) rows and(b+1)c columns.
Choose its smaller whole side as a row/column entry cover. This gives
rank at most bc+min(b,c), without needing the exact binomial theorem.
Across the d^2 blocks the products sum to a^2, and the minima sum to

    3+4(d-1)+2(d-1)^2=2d^2+1=ad-(d-1).

Therefore the exceptional pair has rank at most T-(d-1)<=T-1. This
proves every pair statement in Section1 directly by entry covers,
over any field. The exact pair ranks in PR171 provide optional sharper
calibration; they are not premises of this proof.

## 5. A higher-support witness and its source interpretation

The power(1+xz)^d belongs to S_d: its first d diagonal monomials lie in
the strip, and its last is the extra point. By(1) it has rank T, exceeding
the pairwise ceiling q. All slices of S_d have x-degree at most d and
thus at most T active columns, so T is also its exact maximum rank.
The smaller diagonal(d+1)-coordinate subspace already contains the
same failure of pairwise screening.

For the full canonical A_a tensor, embed g as source parameters
F_(i,0,s)=g_(i,s) and all other middle coordinates zero. The source
coefficient equation requires p+q=0, hence p=q=0. The only nonzero
matrix block is exactly R_a(g). Thus all the rank statements also hold
for these actual source slices, with no discarded unwanted coefficients.

This supplies higher-support rejection witnesses for coordinate
bounded-rank searches. It is not a matrix target, a bounded-rank
ad+1-plane at ceiling q, or an improved arithmetic exponent.

## 6. Why the power witness has a characteristic-zero hypothesis

Positive characteristic can kill the integer Toeplitz determinants.
At a=5,d=2, the middle5-by5 determinant D_5(2,1)=6 vanishes modulo3;
its leading4-minor D_4(2,1)=5 remains nonzero. All other source-level
blocks are invertible triangular matrices. Thus this same power slice
has rank34 over F3, compared with35 over Q or C. All three coefficients
1,2,1 remain nonzero in F3.

This is a negative control for transferring this specific power witness.
It says nothing about the maximum rank of all S_2 over F3. The two-term
rank theorem of PR171 has separate hypotheses and remains field-free.
