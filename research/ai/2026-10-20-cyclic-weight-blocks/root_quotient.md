# A root-of-unity module realizes the candidate vertex character

October 20, 2026. AI-CYCLIC-ROOT-MODULES-053. Written local derivation,
unreviewed. This supplies a target representation, not tensor maps or an
improved exponent. No historical priority is claimed for the representation
facts below.

The [Cartan candidate](../2026-10-20-cyclic-equivariant/a5.md) has formal
vertex characters U=V_(2D)+V_(2D-4) and
W=V_(2D)+V_(2D-2)-V_0, with D=a-1. The negative singlet prevents the
second expression from being a classical SL2 representation. At a primitive
2D-th root q, it becomes an actual quotient representation.

Use the [existing quantum package's convention](../2026-10-20-quantum-recoupling/family.md),
credited to [Arun Ram, Quantum SL2, September 25, 2012](https://soimeme.org/~arunram/Notes/QuantumSL2.html).
The quantum integer is [n]=(q^n-q^-n)/(q-q^-1). In the divided-power basis
of the Weyl module V_m, the generator actions are

    K v_i=q^(m-2i) v_i,
    E v_i=[m-i+1] v_(i-1),
    F v_i=[i+1] v_(i+1).

Take D>=2 and m=2D-2. Since [D]=0, both E and F annihilate v_(D-1),
and K acts on it by one. Thus L=C v_(D-1) is a trivial submodule, and

    Q_D=V_(2D-2)/L

has dimension 2D-2 and character chi(V_(2D-2))-1. This is enough to
realize W=V_(2D) direct sum Q_D, of dimension 4D-1. Taking
U=V=V_(2D) direct sum V_(2D-4) gives dimensions 4D-2 on the other
vertices.

The line also survives the divided-power specialization. Generically,

    E^(k) v_i=[m-i+k choose k]_q v_(i-k),
    F^(k) v_i=[i+k choose k]_q v_(i+k).

On the central vector, for 1<=k<D the relevant coefficient is
[D-1+k choose k]_q. Its numerator contains [D] and its denominator
has no zero at this root. For k>=D the destination index is outside
the module. Hence every positive divided power annihilates L as well.

The extension by L is nonsplit, already for the algebra generated by
E,F,K. The only K-eigenvalue-one vector in V_(2D-2) is the central
vector. An equivariant projection to L would therefore be zero on
all other weight spaces, but E v_D=[D-1] v_(D-1) is nonzero. Such a
projection cannot commute with E.

For the E,F,K algebra alone, the quotient's intervals i<D-1 and i>D-1
are invariant. They are not separate divided-power submodules:
F^(D) sends the class of v_0 to the nonzero class of v_D with coefficient
one. Treating that ordinary-generator splitting as a divided-power
splitting would lose information needed for a root construction.

In the weight indexing 0,...,2D used by the Cartan search, the actual
characters are

    U=(1,1,2,...,2,1,1),
    W=(1,2,...,2,1,2,...,2,1).

At D=2,3,4 these give the vertices of M(6,6,7), M(10,10,11), and
M(14,14,15). The earlier coefficient bounds are necessary conditions
only. This module construction neither supplies maps from the cyclic
source nor makes its generic recoupled tensor globally quantum invariant.
The [prescribed leading quantum flags](../2026-10-20-graded-quantum-flags/graded_obstruction.md)
remain excluded under that note's hypotheses. The quotient does not
reopen those flags.

`root_quotient.py` checks the central divided-power coefficients, quotient
commutator relation, nonsplitting eigenvalue facts, and the crossing
coefficient exactly modulo the cyclotomic polynomial for every D=2,...,10.
These controls use the maintained rational-function quantum arithmetic.
The all-size argument is the elementary calculation above; the finite
checks are not independent mathematical review.
