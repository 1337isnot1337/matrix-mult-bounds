# Sharp dimension caps at the next two source-rank thresholds

Determine the maximum dimension of a complex A4 linear source space
whose every slice has rank strictly below 84, and strictly below 88.
The source staircase family already supplies dimensions 56 and 60
with ranks at most 80 and 84, respectively. Therefore any upper bounds
at those dimensions would be sharp.

The intended proof uses the existing characteristic-zero order-ideal
reduction, small coherent minors, and disjoint pairs of middle blocks.
It must bound every order ideal, with an exhaustive written case split.
The acceptance program must reconstruct the literal source, replay
credited minors, and check the sharp examples without importing the
discovery process.

These source caps can constrain the surviving Laurent target frontier.
A target consequence requires a separate saturated-support argument.
No source-space example alone constructs a matrix-target degeneration,
and no exponent improvement is proposed by this question.
