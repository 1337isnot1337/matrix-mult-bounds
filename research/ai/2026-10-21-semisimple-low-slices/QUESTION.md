# The gap after ordinary low-rank spans

Can a singular degeneration realize the six-punctured-packets-plus-M7
joint target? Its exact conditional gate is2.24. No map has been
constructed. It passes all full Koszul and rank-locus dimension tests,
but the column-span proof excludes arbitrary ordinary linear restrictions.

The smaller4*M7 ->2*M9+M4+M3+M2 trade is now excluded even for general
border degenerations by the separate determinant-factor proof. Its generic
slice determinant is nonzero; the rectangular target's is zero, so the
same factor argument does not apply to the open packet target.

Next useful evidence would be an actual Laurent input map with all source
coefficients checked, or an obstruction whose hypotheses and closedness
cover singular limits. Do not apply the ordinary span argument to border
degenerations. Classical external asymptotic-sum and border-conversion
lemmas retain their credit in the proof.
