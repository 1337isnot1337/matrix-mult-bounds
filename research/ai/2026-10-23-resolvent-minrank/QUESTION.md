# Question and consequence

The initial question was whether PR103's compression-rank hypothesis excludes
an exceptional family of compression pairs.
The proposed route was a uniform minimum-rank bound for its uncompressed
112-dimensional limiting space.

The completed result is a sharp minimum nonzero matrix rank of six.
Its exact certificate reduces the difficult outer block to 31 triangular
six-by-six determinant identities.

The result proves injectivity after a rank loss of at most five.
In particular, every pair of codimension-two compressions preserves dimension 112.
At a total loss of six, a nonzero compression kernel requires a rank-six
direction and the precise alignment in Section 4 of `proof.md`.

The source and original limiting space retain PR103's credit.
No smaller exponent, general singular-target exclusion, or independent review
is claimed. The concurrent regular-long-leg theorem supersedes the narrow
fixed-resolvent target application.
