# Precise research question

Can every complex linear space of canonical A4 source slices of rank
below 64 be bounded in dimension by 35 or less, strongly enough to
improve the prior necessary balanced long-rank sum 176?

The answer obtained here is the sharp dimension bound 32. A new
rank-preserving lowering action reduces any such space to a downward
order ideal. All 611 possible size-33 ideals in the resulting 4-by-3-by-4
box have exact integer rank-64 witnesses. The full coordinate space
with middle coordinate zero or one attains dimension 32 and has every
slice of rank at most 56.

The credited saturated target-support inequality then improves the
necessary balanced long-rank sum to 160. It requires total leading
target deficiency at least 60. No long-map pair at sum 160, complete
Laurent degeneration, or improved exponent is supplied.

This continues the source-space question in the preceding rank-48
frontier package. It changes the reduction from arbitrary coordinate
supports to downward ideals, rather than repeating a numerical search
over the already excluded high-rank long maps.
