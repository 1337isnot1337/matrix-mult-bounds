# Research question and acceptance criterion

Does the dimension-feasible shared-output candidate recorded in
`discovery_input.json` escape tensor rank obstructions that remain valid when
the first degeneration map is singular?

The common-input Full/Mid theorem does not cover shared-output blocks. The
candidate therefore tests a concrete remaining direction, rather than a case
of that theorem. Its shared-leg exterior and symmetric-power tests pass.

This investigation computes the degree-one Koszul flattening on the
40-dimensional second leg. Acceptance requires literal tensor coefficients,
an exact source upper bound, an exact target lower bound, an explicit
decomposition of the full map, and the scope of every basis assumption.

The completed result excludes the canonical candidate, with a rank gap of 21.
Independent first identifications in target blocks remain a distinct question
at this checkpoint. No numerical optimization output is used to accept the
obstruction, and no constructive or asymptotic exponent implication is claimed.
