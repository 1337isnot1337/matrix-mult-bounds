# When is the balanced quadratic rank defect attained?

Started October 23, 2026 from main a510bec after PR218 merged. This
investigation is owner-directed Codex research. No new theorem or exponent
improvement is claimed by this question checkpoint.

PR218 proves that balanced quadratic-z block ranks lie in
{a²,a²+a,a²+2a-1,a²+2a}. The one-below-full value requires primitive
coefficient degree two and indices(a-1,a+1). Its field- and size-dependent
attainment remains unowned; open PR214 concerns consolidation and PR219
concerns constant A4 compressions.

Investigate the degree-(2,2) projective curve cut out by the primitive
biform. The explicit coefficient-kernel map may identify its defect with
H0(C,O_C(a+1,-a-1)); on a smooth genus-one curve this would turn the
question into the order of O_C(1,-1) in the degree-zero Picard group.
Audit the complete coefficient convention, singular/reducible/nonreduced
boundaries, and arbitrary characteristic before claiming this implication.

The saved finite F2 probes at a=6,10 have no defect among264 primitive
quadratics without a fixed root. Preserve those negatives; do not repeat
unchanged searches. Any all-size binary-field pattern requires a proof,
not extrapolation from these finite experiments.

Intended deliverable: a precisely scoped geometric criterion or a corrected
counterexample, exact small controls reconstructed from the literal source,
source versions and hashes, and an explicit next step. No full cyclic-slice
classification, tensor target, independent review or improved exponent is
implied by a one-block criterion.
