# Close the two-coordinate graph charts

The pure-row family and four fixed graph families are closed, with the
partner and first-space restrictions specified in their proofs. The
general mixed five-space remains open.

Every such space has a four-dimensional boundary part W. Some coordinate
two-plane complements W in the six-dimensional maximal boundary frame.
Its mixed row can therefore be represented with at most two nonzero
boundary coordinates. Investigate the fifteen resulting parameterized
charts, keeping their transversality and exceptional strata explicit.

The first axis chart h=t*d0 has a promising rational kernel with only
powers of t in its denominators in discovery calculations. It still
needs a uniform rank certificate, projection analysis and target closure;
that observation is not part of the accepted graph-family theorem.

A fixed minor used for a six-parameter polynomial inverse is not a unit
by the attempted nilpotent argument. Some axis controls have kernel
dimension34 instead of32 at exceptional values. Do not replace those
strata by a generic32-dimensional kernel without a separate proof.

The present local exclusions do not construct a matrix multiplication
algorithm. The exponent-improvement goal and the other workers' source
construction and moving-boundary completion questions remain active.
