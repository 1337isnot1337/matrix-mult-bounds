# Lines inside the saturated A10 four-space

October 23, 2026. Base main `fe602e4`. Owner-directed Codex continuation.

PR120 constructs a saturated quadratic pencil in the moment four-space
`h_3=A,h_7=B,h_11=C,h_15=D` and proves its unique maximal linear extension.
It does not show that other quadratic pencils are equivalent to that seed.

For arbitrary lines inside this four-space, find an exact condition for
the common tangent space to remain four-dimensional. Identify the
exceptional tangent jump, and decide at least one exceptional seed using
higher rank equations rather than tangent dimension alone.

The intended output is a parameter-polynomial certificate and a scoped
linear-extension theorem. Arbitrary quadratic Hankel pencils outside this
four-space, general Segre-image bounds and matrix degenerations are not
assumed settled. Check current PRs before enlarging that scope.
