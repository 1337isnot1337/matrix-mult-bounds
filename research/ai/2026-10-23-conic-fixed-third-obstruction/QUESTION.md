# Does the prescribed full third jet extend?

The exact all-order curve in the preceding fixed-two package preserves
all PR107 map coefficients through order two and its zero first two
kernel corrections. Its third coefficient differs from PR107, whose
third kernel correction has sixteen nonzero entries.

Fix every entry of PR107's B0,...,B3, C0,...,C3 and Y1,Y2,Y3.
For complex a,b,d with d nonzero and c=-d^3, does this complete
incidence third jet extend to order four, or to an all-order curve?

The deliverable is an exact necessary-and-sufficient condition, with
a dual certificate against arbitrary fourth map and kernel coefficients,
and exact curves on every surviving parameter component. Extending a
different choice of the third coefficient does not answer this question.

The result is local rank-twelve incidence of two fourteen-row maps.
It does not determine the full matrix target or the arithmetic exponent.
The prescribed input and source conventions retain PR107 and PR101's
attribution. No independent mathematical review is claimed.
