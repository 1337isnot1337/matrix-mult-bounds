# Positive constructions after the ordinary rectangular frontier

The exponent goal remains active and unbeaten. Existing polynomial and
Hankel theorems already close all ordinary A5 targets; the old minimum-only
frontier must not be treated as the current construction queue.

Among balanced sources through a=8, the sole ordinary single-rectangle
survivor is A8 to M(27,28,28). Its source legs have dimension960 and its
target legs756,784,756. This is a candidate for the existing constructive
source campaign, not a constructed tensor or a proved exponent191/85.
Passing this finite screen is not a sufficient condition for maps.

The quantum worker's separate positive question remains the higher-rank
completion of the exact A4 boundary-shear family. Its current rank14 maps
have first rank12 and do not reach the matrix target. Singular A4 maps
remain open despite the ordinary exclusions.

A productive next contribution needs actual complete coefficient maps,
a genuinely new asymptotic character constraint, or a theorem that removes
an existing premise. Repeating the same necessary tests or rediscovering
an already merged rank-locus theorem is not new mathematical progress.
