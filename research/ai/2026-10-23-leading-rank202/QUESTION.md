# Question

What do the sharp A4 source-space dimensions 68 below slice rank 92
and 72 below slice rank 96 imply for the remaining balanced target
leading ranks after the every-pair bound of 136?

The precise target is any complete complex Laurent A4 to M(10,10,11)
presentation, with arbitrary complex leading kernels, higher coefficients,
and finite Laurent poles on the unselected map. The work must retain
the preceding all-order saturation and every-pair proofs.

The answer is the necessary total bound 202 and, when the short rank
is 100, the bound 28 on each long rank. Exact quotient inequalities
describe which integer pairs survive these new source-cap screens.
The unique numerical total-202 endpoint is (70,66,66), which is not
a target construction. This package does not classify realizable
leading kernels or attempt another finite jet extension.
