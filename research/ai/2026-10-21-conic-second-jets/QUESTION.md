# Complete second-order compatibility on the affine conic

At the affine conic boundary flags of PR94, which nonzero projective normal
classes extend through order two in the rank-twelve incidence of two
fourteen-row maps of the canonical 112-by-112-by-112 source?

Allow every boundary tangent, arbitrary second row coefficients, and a
moving first-source kernel. First determine the complete tangent module,
rather than assuming the common-plane boundary family is exhaustive.

The output is a universal polynomial compatibility criterion and exported
positive two-jets with direct source replay. It is not a matrix target,
an all-order extension, or an exponent improvement. The separate 12-by8
first-extension problem is outside this question.
