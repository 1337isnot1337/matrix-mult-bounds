# Next question: full-window variable coefficients

The written proper-shift-window theorem in `proof.md` bounds the residual needed to reach M(n,m,n) when one variable bulk long-leg map has a common channel-window span of dimension below m and the other map is Toeplitz. For fixed m, sublinear bandwidth and sublinear exceptional-column counts, that residual has border rank at least n^2-o(n^2). Polynomial degrees at most m-2, shared constant-coefficient recurrences of order below m, and low-total-multiplicity exponential polynomials all fall into this class. The result is only about ordinary restrictions and is unreviewed.
