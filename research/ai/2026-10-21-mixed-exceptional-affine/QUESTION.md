# Search inside the exceptional fibers

The15,625-point grid h in{-2,-1,0,1,2}^6 over F_32003 gave one-block
mixed-equation ranks28:15166,27:374,26:21,25:64. The64 sampled rank25
points all lie on the affine family proved in this package. This is a
finite observation and is not a proof that no other rank25 family exists.
Exact rational tangent dimensions at the origin,(1,1,0,0,0,0) and the new
base graph are respectively2,4,3 for their corresponding same-rank loci.

Constant-unit Gaussian elimination of the original six-parameter block
found18 pivots, leaving a20-by26 polynomial residual of degree four.
It did not find a hoped-for26-pivot global reduction. Restricting to the
new affine family still stalled after18 unit pivots. The short left
relations give a simpler exact rank-upper certificate instead.

The generic raw normal system, before selecting a graph, was also tested
at a=b=1. After removing the four planes it has84 variables. Direct
triangular cubic witnesses gave no forced linear cuts. Its stable/projection
split gives necessary families of dimensions54 and48; the projection
square cuts are84->52->48. These are incomplete algebraic reductions,
not actual non-plane solutions or a universal exclusion. This route
should be reopened only with additional source information or a stronger
minor-ideal calculation.

The next constructive question is whether any other point of the affine
family admits a non-plane normal that survives all source stages and
integrates. The present base point fails. Existing integrable boundary
shears have smaller effective first spaces and do not answer this question.
For a target C five-space, every representative h+w of its mixed row must
lie outside claim094's good open set; its boundary W has dimension four.
The new exceptional three-space alone cannot contain that entire affine
four-plane, but other exceptional components and chart factors remain.

Main's concurrent F8 coordinate closure and subgroup-ideal certificates
were inspected and their two focused five-test modules replayed locally.
They retain their original authorship and unreviewed theorem statuses.
They close specified coordinate routes and do not exclude arbitrary
complex group-algebra maps or provide a smaller exponent.
