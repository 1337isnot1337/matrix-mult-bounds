# Arbitrary mixed-row graphs

The b!=0 pure-row case is closed uniformly, including every value of a
after source scaling to b=1. The decisive structural step is the stable
row part and the rank-two projection, not the incomplete large minor search.

Next remove the requirement that V contain m_C(v). A general mixed
five-space contains a row m_C(v)+h, with h in the six-dimensional boundary
annihilator, and has an arbitrary four-dimensional boundary part. The
equations are Z(d)(h,1)=0 and rank Z(d)<=2; setting h=0 is not justified.

One possible route is to prove that every target-admissible non-plane
normal has zero mixed column anyway. If its obstruction has rank two,
the five-space is its entire kernel and would then contain the pure row.
The preceding mixed-hyperplane theorem already handles rank-at-most-one
obstructions with nonzero mixed projection. That additional zero-column
claim is not proved here.

The remaining b=0 chart and exceptional partner endpoints also need
separate treatment. Direct rational and modular Groebner pilots on the
pure-row control timed out, and the incomplete branching search was
replaced by the stable-row argument. A cubic-rank sample was not full.
Those incomplete computations are not mathematical exclusions or proofs.

The exponent remains9/4. A result about a smaller local normal system
must not be promoted as a constructed matrix multiplication algorithm.
