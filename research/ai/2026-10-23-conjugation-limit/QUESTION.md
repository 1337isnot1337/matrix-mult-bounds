# Two-sided singular conjugation of the full A4 source

## Resolution

For the fixed conjugation `I+t^-1 N`, the completed
[universal compression theorem](../2026-10-23-a4-compression-algebra/proof.md)
excludes every constant codimension-two compression, including arbitrary
Laurent first maps and subsequent re-saturation. Every projected leading
space has dimension at least 104, while the complete `M(10,10,11)` target
requires dimension at most 103. This is claim
`AI-A4-ARBITRARY-COMPRESSION-001`, with written and unreviewed status.

Moving compression maps and different singular paths remain outside this
result. The original question below is retained as a historical checkpoint;
its proposed search among constant non-equivariant compressions is closed.
