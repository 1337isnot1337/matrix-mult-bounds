# Two-sided singular conjugation of the full A4 source

October 23, 2026. Owner-directed Codex investigation.

Can a two-sided singular change of basis of the full normalized A4 slice
space, followed by a codimension-two compression, contain the complete
matrix space for M(10,10,11)?

Use the existing unimodular source unit and the normalized coordinate slice
N of rank sixteen with N squared zero. Unlike the earlier one-sided
resolvent, the new family conjugates every source slice by I+sN, with
s=t^-1. Both long maps can therefore have singular leading terms. Permit
arbitrary Laurent first maps. First compute the complete saturated slice
limit, rather than a finite-order incidence jet.

The intended outputs are exact complete limiting slices, a constructive
matrix-unit acceptance condition, and a written analysis of compression
flags. N-equivariant compressions provide a controlled boundary case.
Non-equivariant compressions remain the constructive search question.
No target map, exponent improvement, independent mathematical review, or
formalization is asserted by registering this question.

The source convention and square-zero slice are adopted from the merged
cyclic-source and fixed-resolvent packages. The earlier conic curves have
only fourteen output rows and are not treated as full matrix maps. Open
PRs 103, 156, 168, 178, 188, 193, and 204 were inspected for overlap;
none states the two-sided conjugation-space question above.
