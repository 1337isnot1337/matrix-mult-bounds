# All-size residue units and the sharp singular-space cap

Does the A4 disjoint-unit packing extend to every balanced cyclic source
A_a? Put F(i,k,s)=1 exactly when(i+s) mod a=c and k mod a=d. The full
matrix factors, after reordering, into a permutation matrix and a short
a(2a-1)-square matrix K_c.

Proposed all-field proof: encode a kernel vector of K_c as polynomials
U_0(y),...,U_(2a-2)(y), each of degree at most a-1. Subtract successive
convolution equations to derive a relation with y^a-1. Coprimality with
y and degree bounds should force every U to vanish.

If this proves K_c invertible over every field, its integer determinant
is plus or minus one. The a^2 disjoint supports then give the sharp
all-complex-subspace cap dim W<=a^2(2a-2). Finite checks cannot replace
the all-size recurrence. This is source geometry, not an exponent claim.
