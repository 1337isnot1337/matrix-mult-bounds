# Question and exact stopping condition

Does every complex A4 source space with all slice ranks below 96
have dimension at most 57, as needed for the proposed direct
source-cap continuation from balanced sum144 to sum128?

No. The explicit staircase space in this package has dimension68 and
only92 supported matrix columns. This disproves that premise and
stops the proposed exhaustive search for such a universal cap.

The changed premise for future work is a smaller rank interval or a
restriction to actual two-sided compression kernels with compatible
target support. No general barrier to those refinements is claimed.
