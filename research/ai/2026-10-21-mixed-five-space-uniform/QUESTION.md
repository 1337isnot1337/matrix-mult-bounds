# Remaining five-space equations

The sixteen source combinations e_(2,s,z)-a e_(1,s,z) now have a uniform
exact rank-one certificate. Add their seven row functionals to the C
obstruction. For any actual mixed five-space V, the resulting seven-column
matrix Z(D) has rank at most two and annihilates V.

The next task is to classify these remaining normal directions, including
all boundary graphs. One useful parametrization chooses a mixed vector
e6+h in V, h in C^6. Then Z(D)(h,1)=0, and the six boundary columns have
rank at most two. This includes every mixed V, with redundant choices of h.

Exploratory calculations at a=b=1 leave46 normals after the mixed-row
equations, and36 or32 after low-source equations for two chosen h controls.
These are discovery controls, not a classification of every h. The
rank-two determinantal ideal for the h=0 control has32 effective
parameters and a44-by6 matrix. A direct rational Groebner run on213614
generated minors reached its300-second limit without a result. That is
an incomplete calculation, not a negative mathematical finding.

Try smaller elimination charts, structural factorization or a certified
component decomposition. Preserve exceptional parameters and do not
infer a complex classification from a finite-field calculation. The
coordinate-partner closure and its unproved uniform extension remain
distinct; the exponent goal is still unbeaten.
