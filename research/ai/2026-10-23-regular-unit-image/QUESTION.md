# A regular unit image with one missing long direction

Base: main `539b539dc4eac09114899fd76472aee2d78d9f62`.

Can the all-order regular-unit obstruction for A4 to M(10,10,11) replace
invertibility of the entire limiting source-unit slice by
`rank(S(0) C(0)) >= 109`, where C is the regular full-column-rank long map?

Proposed mechanism: keep 109 independent unit-image columns, allow every
left inverse of their target inclusion, and apply the previous normalized
rank lemma with source defect three. Recover the original long map from
finitely many projections. Then apply the existing ordinary-map exclusion.

Intended output: a general Laurent regularity lemma and its A4 corollary,
with explicit rank margins, written proof, exact finite matrix controls,
source pins and negative controls. The proof must cover every finite pole
order. Finite controls are not a proof of the formal quantifiers.

The source unit itself may be singular. No general singular-long-leg
degeneration, polar unit lift, matrix target or exponent improvement is
claimed. This is a different question from the local conic jet incidence.
