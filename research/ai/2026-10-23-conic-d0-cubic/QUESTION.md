# Exact lifting on the d=0 cubic-conic fiber

October 23, 2026. This continues the pinned PR101 conic source and PR107's
mixed-chart three-jet. Their source and first-jet credit is retained.

Question: when the cubic conic `c=-d^3` is specialized to `c=d=0`, does
PR101's exported polynomial two-jet extend after allowing all seventy
second-coefficient tangent fields? A pole in the mixed-chart formula does
not decide this fiber.

Answer: yes, and the fixed two-jet already lies on an exact polynomial
curve for every complex `a,b`. The second-tangent weights are all zero.
The independent verifier checks the literal source identity and constant
leading minors, without relying on the discovery elimination.

This is a rank-twelve incidence curve for two fourteen-row maps. It does
not construct the full matrix target or improve the exponent. Higher-order
lifting for `d!=0`, other projective base charts and the full matrix target
remain separate questions.
