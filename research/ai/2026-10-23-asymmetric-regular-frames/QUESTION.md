# Unequal partial frames and a target-unit rank upgrade

Can the partial regular frames have different dimensions? The normalized
pole proof has a rectangular output block: its source rank budget is
`2*q_B+q_C`, and its row-kernel bound loses only q_C columns.

After B and F become regular, a target identity upgrades rank B(0) to
the size of the regular C frame, even if its original right frame was
smaller. This could replace the equal109/109 hypothesis by106/110 or
107/109, and lower the full regular unit-image threshold to106.

Develop a general unital theorem, verify every strict inequality, and
scan the complete finite A4 frame-budget range. This is an extension of
PR105 and the partial-frame continuation, not a general construction.
The frames remain hypotheses. All proofs are written and unreviewed.
