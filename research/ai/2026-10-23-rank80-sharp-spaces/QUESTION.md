# Question and accepted output

What is the maximum dimension of an arbitrary complex linear A4 source
space if every slice has rank strictly below 80?

The previous upper bound was 50. The accepted answer is exactly 44.
The upper bound requires complete coordinate-ideal exhaustion after
the credited characteristic-zero lowering reduction. The lower bound
requires an explicit 44-dimensional space with a uniform rank bound,
not sampled low-rank matrices.

The certificate supplies all 1,709 size-45 ideal exclusions, replays
the eleven prior coherent witnesses used to restrict the inventory,
and proves the explicit space has maximum rank 79. Tests must reject
missing ideal coverage, unsupported coefficients, singular minors,
incorrect support covers, changed source pins, and inflated scope.

No target-map rank-pair consequence or exponent improvement is part
of this investigation. The complete rank-80 cap is sharp, so lowering
the dimension bound further would require a different rank threshold
or additional hypotheses on the source space.
