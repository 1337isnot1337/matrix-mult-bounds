# An invariant-slice multiplicity obstruction beyond representation capacity

October 21, 2026. Written proof and exact finite controls, unreviewed.
This obstruction applies to ordinary intertwining maps. It is not
asserted for arbitrary singular degenerations.

## 1. The ordinary rank inequality

Let S be the source SL2 representation and let its trivial isotypic
space have dimension one, generated by e_0. In the weight2l isotypic
spaces, contraction with e_0 gives a bilinear form H_l on the two source
multiplicity spaces, each of dimension m_l. Suppose its rank is r_l.

An ordinary equivariant restriction to a concise matrix target has
injective maps on every target multiplicity space. If the first target
leg has a one-dimensional trivial component, its image is a nonzero
multiple of e_0. Write t_B,l and t_C,l for the other target multiplicities,
and rho_l for the rank of the target's corresponding trivial-leg pairing.
Restricting a rank-r_l form by these two injections gives

    rho_l >= t_B,l + t_C,l + r_l - 2m_l.             (1)

This is just the rank loss bound from deleting rows and columns. It is
stronger than separately checking t_B,l,t_C,l<=m_l.

For multiplicity-free matrix vertices U,V,W with exactly one common
irreducible V_alpha in U and V, the first target trivial component is
its identity map. The target pairing rank rho_l is the number of
irreducibles V_gamma in W for which V_(2l) occurs in V_alpha tensor
V_gamma. The trace pairing on each such irreducible Hom channel is
nondegenerate. This supplies every quantity in (1) by integer
Clebsch-Gordan arithmetic.

Crucially, a restricted matrix rank can drop in a singular limit. The
argument therefore does not exclude a Laurent degeneration merely from
failure of (1). Representation-capacity exclusions and the separately
proved shared-top-irrep polynomial obstruction have different scopes.

## 2. An exact source basis and contraction

For the balanced source A(a,a), put d=a-1. Its physical matrices are

    W_F[(i,q),(p,s)] = F_(i,p+q,s),  0<=i,p,q,s<a.

The standard diagonal action has weight2(k-i-s) on F_(i,k,s). A
highest-weight2l vector has coefficients f_(i,s) at k=i+s+l. The
raising-operator equation is

    (i+1)f_(i+1,s)+(s+1)f_(i,s+1)
        =(i+s+l+1)f_(i,s).

Set one unit value on the boundary i+s=2d-l and the other boundary
values zero, and solve downwards. These vectors form a basis of the
highest-weight multiplicity space: they are independent by their
boundary values, and their number is
m_l=min(l+1,2a-1-l), the Clebsch-Gordan multiplicity.

The l=0 vector is the unique invariant, with coefficients

    c_(i,s)=binom(d,i) binom(d,s) / binom(2d,i+s).

The Weyl reflection sends a highest vector to the lowest vector with
coefficients (-1)^l f_(d-i,d-s), at k=i+s-l. The script evaluates
trace(W_c W_high W_low) by physical weight blocks of size at most a.
This is the original tensor contraction, reorganized into smaller blocks.
For a=2,3,4 every channel is also replayed by the independent six-index
original-source sum.

All computation is modulo65519, with denominators invertible for the
reported sizes. A full-rank modular matrix certifies the rank of its
rational lift. The stored a=24 certificate gives full rank m_l in all47
channels, hence total invariant-slice rank27072 over Q and C. No floating
rank threshold is involved. No all-size nonsingularity theorem is claimed.

## 3. A capacity-passing lead that fails the slice test

The restricted search uses multiplicity-free physical vertex subspaces:
subsets of the odd dimensions1,3,...,47, with at most five summands and
total dimension124. There are190 such vertices. Their capacity graph has
four unordered compatible triples. One has the top irrep47 at all three
vertices and is already excluded by AI-CYCLIC-EQUIVARIANT-050.

The other three pass representation capacity but violate (1). For example,

    U=(1,35,41,47), V=(9,23,45,47), W=(17,29,33,45).

The U,V trivial component is their shared47. At l=1, the B and C target
multiplicities are both2, while m_1=r_1=2. The target pairing has rank1,
whereas (1) requires at least2. The other two triples have analogous
witnesses at l=1 and l=2. The exact graph and witness replay is separate
from the source contraction certificate.

Thus this entire bounded physical-subset ansatz is excluded for ordinary
A(24,24) -> M(124,124,124). This is not an exhaustive search of all vertex
representations, and does not eliminate the three leads as singular
degenerations. Passing capacity alone never supplied target maps or a
smaller exponent.
