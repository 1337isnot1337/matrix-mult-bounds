# Two linear polynomial relations at one-unit slice excess

October21,2026. Written proof, unreviewed. Necessary structure, no target maps.

Assume 2<=a<=b. Let a source slice have full scalar-symbol rank a,
crossed coefficient rank at least a+1, and source rank at most a^2*b+1.
Then its crossed rank is exactly a+1 and its source rank is exactly
 a^2*b+1. Moreover both polynomial-span stages in the maintained source
rank argument have a unique primitive polynomial relation of degree one.
This is a structural constraint on the one-unit stratum, not its complete
classification or an exponent improvement.

## Polynomial relation lemma

Let S have a basis of r+1 vector polynomials over a field K. Assume their
rank over K(t) is r. Their polynomial relations form a free module of rank
one generated by a primitive vector c(t): clear denominators from the
unique rational relation and divide the common polynomial factor.
Every polynomial relation is a polynomial multiple of c, since its entries
have gcd1. Put delta=max degree(c_i). Constant independence implies
delta>=1. Therefore for T>=1 the shifted span has dimension

    dim(S+tS+...+t^(T-1)S)
      = T*(r+1)-max(0,T-delta).                      (1)

Indeed, the coefficient-map kernel consists exactly of multiples h*c
with degree(h)<T-delta. Polynomial degrees add in a component of maximum
degree, so there is no hidden cancellation in this count. If T>=2 and
the dimension is T*r+1, then delta=1. Its constant and linear coefficient
vectors are independent; otherwise primitivity would give a constant
relation between the basis vectors.

## Equality through both source stages

Use the notation of `quantitative_crossed_rank.md`. Let d be crossed rank,
rho the rank over C(x) of K(x), D the constant span dimension of its
polynomial columns, and R the C(z)-rank of the inner vector-polynomial
span S. The established bounds are

    rank M >= D+(b-1)*rho,
    rho>=a^2,
    D>=d+(a-1)*R,
    R>=a,
    rank M >= (ab-1)*a+d.

The last bound first forces d=a+1 and rank M=a^2*b+1. Since also
rank M>=b*rho and b>=2, the integer rho must equal a^2. The first and
third bounds then force D=a^2+1 and R=a.

The inner S has dimension a+1 and rank a, and its a-fold shifted span
has dimension D=a^2+1. Formula(1), with T=a, gives delta=1.

Choose a constant basis of the polynomial columns of K(x). This outer
span has dimension a^2+1 and rank a^2. Its b-fold shifted span is exactly
the image of M^T, of dimension a^2*b+1. Formula(1), now with T=b, again
gives delta=1. Both assertions are basis-independent under constant basis
changes.

For the remaining A(8,10) ordinary candidate, any rank-two parameter
whose crossed rank is9 must therefore have these two linear relations.
Crossed rank8 is a separate remaining case. The known rank641 slice is
compatible with this conclusion and is retained as a sharp control.

## Sharp controls and provenance

The model span {e0,t*e0,e1,...,e_(r-1)} has shifted dimension T*r+1
and primitive relation (t,-1,0,...,0). Replacing t*e0 by t^2*e0 gives
degree two and shifted dimension T*r+2 for T>=2; it does not pass the
one-unit equality test. The finite verifier checks the coefficient maps
and both source-stage sizes r=8,T=8 and r=64,T=10.

The polynomial relation argument is an elementary PID calculation included
above. The application uses the previously credited source growth lemma.
No historical-priority, independent-review or formalization claim is made.
