# A second spin-two copy is obstructed equivariantly

For A33 and target M(5,5,6), choose vertex representations V4,V4,2V2.
The source and both non-first target legs have V6 multiplicity two. The
first leg has unique V0 and V8 components. Their coefficient pencils
therefore transform by scalar left-right equivalence just as in the
five-dimensional asymmetric pencil proof.

Exact calculation gives source H=G0^-1 G8 with trace27 and trace-square702,
whereas the target H is the two-dimensional identity. Thus the scale-free
ratios are26/27 and1/2, respectively. With Q=adj(G0)G8 the polynomial
27 tr(Q²)-26 tr(Q)² vanishes on all equivariant images and their closure,
but is -50 on the target. The matrices and exact reconstruction are saved
in `double-spin-pencil.json` and `double_spin_pencil.py`.

This excludes this proposed duplication of the rectangular seed, even if
all its equivariant maps change or depend on a degeneration parameter.
It does not exclude non-equivariant M(5,5,6) maps, or M(5,5,4) with third
vertex V2+V0. It is an unreviewed symmetry-class obstruction, with no
improved exponent or historical-priority claim.
