# Research question

Does the affine all-order conic normal-class criterion remain complete at
every point of the projective conic base, with the correct transition of
normal directions and without losing the leading row or flattening ranks?

The source parameter space is the product of the common-factor conic and the
two residual projective lines. The preceding affine theorem covers only the
chart in which the conic parameter and both residual constants are finite.

The answer is yes: the exact global equation is

\[
(a_1r-a_0s)(b_1r+b_0s)(s\lambda^3+r\mu^3)=0.
\]

The package proves the missing tangent and curvature strata, identifies the
normal transition under a literal source symmetry, and supplies exact
polynomial curves. Boundary first velocities are free; no prescribed full
jet is asserted to extend. The result is a local incidence theorem and does
not construct a matrix multiplication target.
